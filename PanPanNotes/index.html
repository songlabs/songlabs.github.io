PanPanNotes Privacy Policy

This policy describes PanPanNotes version 1.0.

PanPanNotes records audio and helps you transcribe, translate, review and prepare meeting notes on your iPhone.

Information used on your device

The app stores recordings or audio files you import, meeting titles, transcripts, translations, manually assigned speaker names, highlights, terminology rules, meeting notes, source references, note versions and preferences in its local app storage. You decide what to record or import. Obtain any required recording permission and avoid recording or sharing information you are not authorized to use.

The app has no user account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated upload service. It does not send meeting recordings or text to a developer-operated server and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Microphone and Apple processing

Microphone access is used when you start recording, including a short recording test. Recording can continue in the background while active. You can stop recording in the app and manage microphone permission in iOS Settings. The microphone captures nearby audible sound; the app does not log into conferencing services or access a computer’s internal audio.

Supported transcription and translation use Apple’s device-based speech and translation frameworks. Missing language assets can be downloaded through Apple services when you choose to proceed. Availability depends on the device, language and installed assets. These system downloads can use the network and are governed by Apple’s services and privacy practices.

Optional automatic meeting notes use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. This feature is off by default and requires your enablement and confirmation of the input. It requires a supported device and language, enabled Apple Intelligence and a ready model. This version does not use Private Cloud Compute or another cloud inference service for meeting notes. You can create notes manually when automatic generation is unavailable. Automatically generated information may be inaccurate and should be reviewed.

Local storage, retention and deletion

The app’s data directory is excluded from system backup and uses iOS Data Protection. The app does not provide cloud synchronization. Keep separate copies of important recordings or exports. Loss of the device or removal of the app may cause loss of local records.

Records remain in app storage until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting a meeting removes that meeting’s local app folder, including its audio and related records. App-wide preferences and terminology rules are separate from individual meetings. Removal of the app normally removes its local container. This does not delete source files you imported from elsewhere or copies you have exported or shared.

Importing and sharing

You choose audio files using the system file picker. A file provider may retrieve a file from its own cloud service according to your settings. PanPanNotes copies the selected audio into its local storage for use in the app.

When you export audio, transcripts or meeting notes, the app opens the iOS share sheet. Only you choose the destination or recipient. Exported content may include meeting text, speaker names, timestamps, highlights or note references, depending on the export selected. The chosen app, recipient or service handles the copy under its own practices. PanPanNotes cannot retract copies already shared.

Apple and support services

Apple may process information independently when providing the App Store, operating system, language assets or other Apple services. This policy describes PanPanNotes’ app behavior and does not replace Apple’s privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information under its own privacy statement. If you contact the support email address, your email service and the receiving email service process your message and the information you choose to include. The app does not automatically attach or send your recordings or transcripts to support.

Contact

For PanPanNotes support or privacy questions, contact SongLabs at songlabs.dev@gmail.com. Please do not send meeting recordings, transcripts or other confidential information with a support request.

PanPanNotes 隐私政策

本政策说明 PanPanNotes 1.0 版本。

PanPanNotes 用于在 iPhone 上录音，并帮助您转写、翻译、回听校对和编写会议纪要。

在设备上使用的信息

应用在本地应用存储中保存录音或您导入的音频、会议标题、转写文字、译文、手动指定的说话人姓名、重点标记、术语规则、纪要、来源引用、纪要版本和偏好设置。由您决定录制或导入什么内容。请取得所需录音许可，不要录制或分享您无权使用的信息。

应用不设用户账号系统，不含广告 SDK 或分析 SDK，也不设开发者运营的上传服务。应用不会将会议录音或文字发送至开发者运营的服务器，也不会跨其他公司的应用或网站跟踪您。

麦克风与 Apple 处理功能

您主动开始录音或短时试录时，应用使用麦克风。已开始的录音可在后台继续。您可在应用内结束录音，并在 iOS 设置中管理麦克风权限。麦克风采集附近可听见的声音；应用不会登录会议服务，也不会直接读取电脑内部音频。

受支持的转写和翻译使用 Apple 的设备端语音与翻译框架。当缺少语言资源时，您可以选择通过 Apple 服务下载。可用性取决于设备、语言和已安装资源。系统下载可能使用网络，并受 Apple 服务及其隐私政策约束。

可选的自动纪要使用 Apple 设备端 Foundation Models 框架。此功能默认关闭，需要您启用并确认输入内容，还需要受支持的设备与语言、已开启的 Apple Intelligence 和就绪的模型。本版本不使用 Private Cloud Compute 或其他云端推理服务生成纪要。自动生成不可用时，您仍可手动编写。自动生成的内容可能不准确，请核对。

本地存储、保留与删除

应用数据目录排除在系统备份之外，并使用 iOS 数据保护。应用不提供云同步。请为重要录音或导出文件另行保留副本。设备丢失或删除应用可能导致本地记录丢失。

记录保存在应用存储中，直至您将其删除或移除应用。删除会议会移除该会议的本地应用文件夹，包括音频与关联记录。应用级偏好和术语规则独立于单个会议。移除应用通常会移除其本地容器，但不会删除您从其他位置导入的原文件，也不会删除已导出或分享的副本。

导入与分享

您通过系统文件选择器选择音频。文件提供方可能根据您的设置从其云服务取回文件。PanPanNotes 将所选音频复制到应用本地存储以供使用。

导出音频、转写或纪要时，应用会打开 iOS 系统分享面板，由您选择目的地或收件人。根据所选导出内容，文件可能包含会议文字、说话人姓名、时间戳、重点或纪要引用。所选应用、收件人或服务按其自身规则处理副本。PanPanNotes 无法撤回已分享的副本。

Apple 与支持服务

Apple 在提供 App Store、操作系统、语言资源或其他 Apple 服务时，可能独立处理信息。本政策说明 PanPanNotes 应用的行为，不替代 Apple 的隐私政策。本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能依据其隐私声明处理连接信息。如果您通过支持邮箱联系我们，您使用的邮件服务及接收邮件的服务会处理邮件及您主动提供的信息。应用不会自动向支持邮箱附加或发送录音、转写内容。

联系方式

如需 PanPanNotes 支持或有隐私问题，请通过 songlabs.dev@gmail.com 联系 SongLabs。请勿在支持请求中发送会议录音、转写内容或其他保密信息。

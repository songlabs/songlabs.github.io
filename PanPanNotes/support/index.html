Version 1.0.1 support

Requires iOS 26 or later; the Watch app requires watchOS 26 or later. No login is required. Start with a short, non-sensitive recording or audio import. The microphone captures nearby sound, not internal computer audio. Check microphone permission in Settings.

Transcription depends on Apple device/language support and installed assets. Confirm a download in the app if needed. Keep the audio and review the text; background or complete transcription is not guaranteed. Candidate text is separate and can be shared separately. Saved transcript history is available from the recording details.

During recording, reopening the app returns to the current recording. Stop normally before listening back. Watch transfers may wait for connectivity and system scheduling; keep the Watch original until you have checked the iPhone copy. Explicitly deleting the corresponding phone record in the updated version also cleans its received Watch audio and staging files; the Watch original is unchanged. Old transfer copies whose main records were already deleted are not automatically cleaned and have no separate in-app cleanup entry. Deletion is not atomic across folders; failure or interruption does not guarantee complete recovery.

Version 1.0.1 no longer offers AI/manual meeting-note creation, translation, glossary, speaker or highlight controls. Existing stored fields are not automatically erased by this change. Version 1.0 help remains below for users of 1.0.

Export important audio and text before removing the app. There is no developer-operated recovery copy or cloud sync. If asked for diagnostics, use Settings → Recording diagnostics → Share recording diagnostics. Review the recipient before sharing. Diagnostics do not include recordings or transcripts and are not automatically sent.

For support, email songlabs.dev@gmail.com with app/system version, device model, language and a non-sensitive description. Do not send confidential recordings, transcripts, passwords or secrets.

1.0.1 지원

iOS 26 이상, Watch 앱은 watchOS 26 이상이 필요합니다. 로그인은 필요하지 않습니다. 민감한 정보가 없는 짧은 녹음이나 오디오 가져오기로 테스트하세요. 마이크는 주변 소리를 녹음하며 컴퓨터 내부 오디오를 직접 가져오지 않습니다. 설정에서 마이크 권한을 확인할 수 있습니다.

전사는 Apple의 기기·언어 지원과 설치된 리소스에 따라 달라집니다. 필요하면 앱에서 다운로드를 확인하세요. 오디오를 보관하고 텍스트와 대조하세요. 백그라운드 처리나 완전한 전사를 보장하지 않습니다. 후보 텍스트를 별도로 공유할 수 있으며 녹음 상세 화면에서 저장된 전사 기록을 확인할 수 있습니다.

녹음 중 앱을 다시 열면 현재 녹음 화면으로 돌아갑니다. 정상적으로 종료한 후 다시 들으세요. Watch 전송은 연결과 시스템 처리를 기다릴 수 있습니다. iPhone 사본을 확인할 때까지 Watch 원본을 보관하세요. iPhone에는 Watch에서 받은 오디오의 전송 사본도 저장됩니다. 이 업데이트 버전에서 해당 iPhone 녹음을 명시적으로 삭제하면 관련 수신 오디오와 임시 파일을 먼저 제거한 후 기본 기록을 삭제합니다. 모든 전송 사본을 자동으로 정리하는 작업은 아닙니다. iPhone 기록을 삭제해도 Watch 원본은 삭제되지 않습니다.

이전 버전에서 기본 기록을 삭제한 후 남은 전송 사본은 자동 정리되지 않으며 별도의 정리 기능도 없습니다. 삭제는 폴더 간 원자적 작업이 아니며 모든 실패 후 완전한 복구를 보장하지 않습니다. 1.0.1에서는 AI/수동 회의록 작성, 번역, 용어집, 화자, 중요 표시 기능의 진입점을 제공하지 않습니다. 이 변경으로 저장된 필드가 자동 삭제되지는 않습니다. 아래의 1.0 도움말은 1.0 사용자에게 적용됩니다.

Getting started

PanPanNotes requires iOS 26 or later. No app account or sign-in is required. Start with a short recording test, or import your own audio file. Obtain any required recording permission before recording other people.

Why can’t I hear the meeting in the recording?

The iPhone microphone records nearby audible sound. It cannot directly capture a computer’s internal audio or sound confined to headphones. Play the meeting through a nearby loudspeaker and use the short recording test to check the result. Check microphone permission in iOS Settings.

Why are transcription or translation unavailable?

Availability depends on Apple’s device and language support and installed language assets. Use the in-app download confirmation when assets are missing; a network connection may be needed. Recording and transcription are separate, so check the saved audio even if text processing fails. Background transcription and translation are not guaranteed.

Why are automatic meeting notes unavailable?

Automatic notes are off by default. They require your opt-in, confirmation of input, a compatible device and language, enabled Apple Intelligence and a ready model. Availability can vary by region. Use manual source-backed notes when the automatic feature is unavailable. Review generated notes against the transcript and recording.

Are speakers identified automatically?

No. This version supports manually assigning, renaming and merging speakers. Automatic speaker identification is unavailable.

How do I export or delete a meeting?

Open the meeting details to export audio or text through the iOS share sheet. Choose the destination yourself. Deleting a meeting removes its local app records and audio; it does not remove source files or previously exported copies. Preserve important files before deleting anything.

Can I recover records after deleting the app or losing my phone?

This version has no cloud synchronization, and the app data directory is excluded from system backup. There is no developer-operated copy of your meeting records to restore. Export important recordings and text to a destination you control before removing the app.

开始使用

PanPanNotes 需要 iOS 26 或更高版本，无需注册或登录。可先进行短时试录，或导入自己的音频文件。录制他人声音前请取得所需许可。

为什么录不到会议声音？

iPhone 麦克风采集附近可听见的声音，不能直接读取电脑内部音频或只在耳机中播放的声音。请通过附近的扬声器播放会议，并用短时试录确认效果。在 iOS 设置中检查麦克风权限。

为什么转写或翻译不可用？

可用性取决于 Apple 对设备和语言的支持，以及已安装的语言资源。缺少资源时请按应用内提示确认下载，下载可能需要联网。录音与文字处理相互独立，处理失败时请先检查已保存的音频。不保证后台转写和翻译持续运行。

为什么自动纪要不可用？

自动纪要默认关闭，需要主动启用并确认输入，还需要受支持的设备与语言、已开启的 Apple Intelligence 和就绪的模型。不同地区的可用性可能不同。不可用时可根据转写内容手动编写带引用的纪要。请对照录音和原文检查生成结果。

能自动识别说话人吗？

不能。本版本支持手动分配、重命名和合并说话人，自动说话人识别暂不可用。

如何导出或删除会议？

在会议详情中通过 iOS 系统分享面板导出音频或文字，并自行选择目的地。删除会议会移除该会议的本地记录和音频，但不会删除原始导入文件或已经导出的副本。删除前请保留重要文件。

删除应用或丢失手机后能恢复记录吗？

本版本不提供云同步，应用数据目录排除在系统备份之外。开发者没有可用于恢复的会议记录副本。移除应用前，请将重要录音和文字导出至您自行管理的位置。

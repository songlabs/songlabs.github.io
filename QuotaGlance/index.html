Usage quotas at a glance

QuotaGlance

A local-first app for viewing the remaining 5-hour and Weekly usage windows returned by connected Codex and Claude services on iPhone, iPad, widgets, and Apple Watch.

Get Support Read the Privacy Policy

Codex and Claude

Connect your own supported provider account and view the quota windows supplied by that provider. Provider availability and compatibility may change.

Local-first

OAuth credentials remain in the iPhone or iPad Keychain. QuotaGlance has no developer-operated backend, analytics, tracking, advertising SDK, or remote usage-history database.

Widgets and Apple Watch

Widgets, the Apple Watch app, and complications use non-credential snapshots. They do not receive OAuth tokens or make provider OAuth and usage requests.

Free, Trial, and Pro

Free access remains available. The optional seven-day Trial does not renew or charge automatically. Lifetime Pro is a separate one-time purchase handled by Apple.

Independent third-party application

QuotaGlance is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI or Anthropic. The provider names are used only to identify services a user may choose to connect.

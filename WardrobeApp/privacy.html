WardrobeApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

WardrobeApp ("the App") is an iPhone application for organizing clothing and generating outfit suggestions. WardrobeApp is designed to keep wardrobe-related information primarily on the user's device and to minimize unnecessary collection of personal data.

1. Wardrobe Data

WardrobeApp stores information such as clothing records, profile settings, outfit history, categories, colors, seasons, styles, notes, and other wardrobe-related information locally on your device.

WardrobeApp does not upload this wardrobe information to a developer-operated server.

2. Clothing Images

Images added to WardrobeApp are stored in the App's local storage on your device.

WardrobeApp does not upload your clothing images to a developer-operated server.

3. Camera

Camera access is used only when you choose to photograph a clothing item for your wardrobe.

Camera permission can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Photo Selection

When you choose an existing image, WardrobeApp uses Apple's system photo picker. The App receives access only to the image you explicitly select through the picker.

WardrobeApp does not require unrestricted access to your entire Photo Library for this feature.

5. Clothing Image Analysis

WardrobeApp may analyze clothing images to assist with functions such as foreground extraction, clothing classification, and color identification.

This processing is performed on your device. WardrobeApp does not send clothing images to a developer-operated AI service or server for this analysis.

6. Outfit Recommendations

WardrobeApp generates outfit recommendations using wardrobe information stored on your device.

Your wardrobe records, clothing images, profile information, and outfit history are not uploaded to a developer-operated server for recommendation processing.

7. Apple Weather

WardrobeApp uses Apple Weather / WeatherKit to obtain weather information for fixed Tokyo reference coordinates and uses this information as context for outfit recommendations.

WardrobeApp does not request access to your current location. Your wardrobe data, clothing images, profile information, and outfit history are not included in WeatherKit requests.

Apple services are governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

8. Advertisements

The free version of WardrobeApp may display banner advertisements using Google Mobile Ads SDK.

WardrobeApp does not provide your wardrobe records, clothing images, profile information, or outfit history to Google for advertising purposes.

Google Mobile Ads may process advertising-related, device, diagnostic, or consent information according to Google's own policies and the user's applicable privacy choices.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Analytics

WardrobeApp does not currently use a separate third-party analytics service to analyze your wardrobe activity.

10. Accounts and Cloud Storage

WardrobeApp does not require a WardrobeApp account.

The App does not currently provide a developer-operated cloud synchronization service for wardrobe records or clothing images.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Wardrobe information and clothing images remain stored locally until they are deleted by the user or removed as part of normal app data deletion.

You can delete individual wardrobe items and history from within WardrobeApp. You can also delete the App to remove locally stored application data, subject to normal iOS storage behavior.

12. Third-Party Services

WardrobeApp may communicate with the following third-party platform services for specific app functionality:

  • Apple Weather / WeatherKit — weather information
  • Google Mobile Ads — banner advertising and advertising consent handling

These services operate under their respective privacy policies and terms.

13. Sale of Personal Data

WardrobeApp does not sell your wardrobe information, clothing images, profile information, or outfit history.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WardrobeApp's features, third-party services, or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

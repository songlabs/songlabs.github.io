WardrobeApp Support

WardrobeApp is an iPhone wardrobe organizer that helps you manage your clothes and create practical outfit suggestions from items stored on your device.

Getting Started

You can add clothing from the Wardrobe screen by taking a photo with the camera or selecting an image using Apple's system photo picker.

Saved clothing information can be edited manually, including:

  • Category
  • Color
  • Season
  • Style
  • Notes

Outfit Recommendations

After adding clothing and completing the required profile information, WardrobeApp can generate outfit suggestions using items stored in your wardrobe.

Weather information is used as additional context for outfit recommendations. WardrobeApp currently uses Apple Weather data for fixed Tokyo reference coordinates and does not request your current location.

Where is my data stored?

Wardrobe records, profile settings, outfit history, and clothing images are stored locally on your device.

WardrobeApp does not require an account and does not upload your wardrobe data or clothing images to a developer-operated server.

Camera and Photos

Camera permission is requested only when you choose to photograph a clothing item.

When selecting an existing image, WardrobeApp uses Apple's system photo picker. The app does not require full access to your Photo Library for this operation.

Advertisements

The free version of WardrobeApp may display banner advertisements using Google Mobile Ads.

WardrobeApp does not send your wardrobe records or clothing images to advertising services.

Deleting Data

Individual clothing items and outfit history can be deleted from within the app.

Deleting WardrobeApp from your device removes the app's locally stored data, subject to normal iOS storage behavior.
